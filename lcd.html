Virtual LCD

The machine had a CrystalFontz 20×4 backlit LCD. It cycled between the time, current contents, and the last six purchases.

The green display in the sidebar recreates that behavior. Select “Change display” to switch between the saved purchase list, saved inventory, and your local time. It is a browser demonstration, with no connection to a vending machine.
