The CokePuter has moved! (physcially)

Jason moved outta Laurel, and he took the CokePuter with him. A big thanks to all the people who helped me move this huge honking thing! So it's now happily living in Claymont, DE. A new picture will be up shortly...

 

be patient, this page being served by the CokePuter, a frankensteined compaq486 with 8megs running Apache (because MS PWS is unsafe and unweildy)

Welcome to our CokePuter page! Revel in our Nerdiness! Gawk at our Slothfulness! Gape slack-jawed at our wasting of technology! Shudder in our wanton abuses of hardware/software just to monitor vending machine activity!

Original photo unavailable: CokeputerInDE.jpg

 

Current Hardware:
80486, 8megs ram, 200meg HDD (60meg free), soundblaster 8bit, CrystalFontz 20x4 LCD ArrayBacklight Display and a LogitecCamera that uses the serial port and keyboard port instead of a USB port (since the cokeputer doesn't have a USB port). But as you'll read in old news, it doesn't matter because I wrote my caputer code using TWAIN and this cam doesn't like...

Software:
Win95, CokeMachine 1.4 software (my own stuff), Apache 1.3.22, WinVNC (for remote admin)

This is New CokeMachine, since I fried the motherboard of Classic CokeMachine by accidently running 110vac thru it. When this happens you'll see the under destruction page instead of CokePuter

 

 

OLD NEWS ITEMS

PointlessCode added!<16dec02>
Found a cool sunrise/sunset calculator c++ class online, so integrated it into the cokeputer. Now the LCD display backlight turns on at sunset, off at sunrise. In theory I could make the virtual LCD turn grey-background during the day to reflect this. Okay, maybe when I get more free time ;-p

Spent a coupla mins and fixed all the VirtualLCD. Have to play with cleaning up the black table lines...

<Dangnibit>Machine doesn't like it's TWAIN drivers</Dangnibit>18sept02

Finally found and implemented the code so the cokeputer could twain in images from a cam. Got it working just fine on my dev machine. So xported it to the cokeputer, but no dice. Turns out that for Win95 the Logitech VCcam doesn't like twain, it's got its own custom OCX that'll just make me angry. This would actually make it worth upgrading from my (very funny) 8meg 486... I'm almost half temped to install the other cam and OCX's just to get the software working for migrating to the CokePuter. Almost ;-p

 

<woohoo>The LCD Display's not dead after all!</woohoo>18sept02

Turns out it was just having issues. I did a big reboot tonite while trying to get twain to work, and it came back on. Silly technology...

<sigh>The LCD Display's gone all kaplooie</sigh>12sept02

We had a power outtage. So nothing new, the machine reboots and detects problems with the FAT tables due to apache (still haven't figured that one out yet, but happens after every hard reboot). But later I noticed that the LCD Display was blank. Backlit, but nothing was popping up. So one of these nights I'll pull it and test it on my development machine to find out if it's the display ($45) or com1 port (we've got lots of extra 486 motherboards in the basement).

Months of Continuous Operation (woohoo!) 3aug02

While I was gone for six weeks in the S Pacific, I thought for sure it was going to crash. It usually does. Normally around five mins after I'm out the door. But to my surprise it worked just fine the whole time I was gone. Of course the first night I'm back one of the juice cans turns sideways and jams the machine, but otherwise just fine (jp and I fixed it with a cookie tray and steak knife). So I'm pretty proud of the machine!
